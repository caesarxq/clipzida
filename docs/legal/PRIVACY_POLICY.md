# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 20 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information CLIPZIDA handles when you use our
website or interact with our content on social platforms (the "Service").

## 1. The short version

We are a small clip-publication account. **We do not run analytics, we do not set
tracking cookies, and we do not collect personal data from visitors.** The only
data involved is the account information you hold with the platforms we publish on
(YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, X, Discord), which is governed by those platforms'
privacy policies.

## 2. Information we collect

**We do not collect information directly from you.** We have no accounts, no
newsletter signup, no forms, no checkout, and no analytics scripts.

We do not collect:
- names, email addresses, or contact details;
- IP addresses or device identifiers (nothing is logged on our side);
- cookies, pixels, or similar tracking technologies.

## 3. Information handled by third parties

We publish content on third-party platforms. Those platforms collect their own
information when you use them, for their own purposes. Their privacy practices are
described in their own policies:

- YouTube / Google — https://policies.google.com/privacy
- TikTok — https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/eea/privacy-policy/en
- Discord — https://discord.com/privacy
- Instagram / Meta — https://privacy.facebook.com/
- X — https://privacy.x.com/

**We are not responsible for how those platforms handle your data.**

## 4. Information we may receive

The platforms provide us with **aggregate, non-identifying performance metrics**
for our published posts (for example, view counts). We cannot identify individuals
from this data.

If you email us at **clipzida@gmail.com**, we will hold your message content and
address solely to respond to you. We do not add you to any marketing list and do
not share your address with third parties.

## 5. Data sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. We do not share information with third
parties except:
- as required by law or a valid legal process;
- to protect the rights, property, or safety of CLIPZIDA or others.

## 6. Data retention and security

We retain platform-provided metrics only as long as they are useful to the
operation of the Service. Correspondence is retained only as long as needed to
resolve your enquiry. We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to
protect any information we hold.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (for example under GDPR or Kenya's Data Protection
Act, 2019), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the
processing of your personal data, or to object to processing. Because we collect
almost nothing, such requests will generally relate to correspondence. Contact us
at **clipzida@gmail.com** to exercise any right.

## 8. Children

The Service is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly target
children. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child
has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above
will change accordingly.

## 10. Contact

Privacy questions: **clipzida@gmail.com**.

## 11. Governing law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of the Republic of Kenya, including the
Data Protection Act, 2019.
